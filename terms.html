1. Your account

You connect your own TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit. You can disconnect it at any time in TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).

2. Posting

Clip Studio never posts without you. For every video you see a preview, edit the caption, choose who can view it, choose interaction settings and press “Post to TikTok”. Videos are posted only to the account you connected.
