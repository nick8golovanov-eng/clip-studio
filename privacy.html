Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Clip Studio is a desktop application that runs on the creator's own computer. This policy explains what data it uses.

Data we access

  • From TikTok (with your permission): your open ID, display name, username and avatar (user.info.basic), and permission to upload and post the videos you approve (video.upload, video.publish).
  • From Twitch: public live streams, public chat messages and public clips, used to find highlights.

How we use it

Your TikTok profile info is shown on the posting screen so you know which account you post to. Access tokens are used only to upload the videos you approve. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Where data is stored

All data (access tokens, rendered videos, captions) is stored locally on your own computer. Clip Studio has no server and keeps no copy of your data. Videos you post are sent directly to TikTok's API.

Retention and deletion

Rendered videos are deleted from your computer after 72 hours. You can revoke Clip Studio's access at any time in TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions); deleting the app folder removes all local data.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: message the Clip Studio TikTok account @streamclipsdaily100.
