Clip Studio

Clip Studio turns the best moments of Twitch streams into vertical short videos and helps creators publish them to their own TikTok and YouTube Shorts accounts.

How it works

  • Watches public Twitch streams and chat and detects moments where chat reacts (laughing, hype, shock).
  • Cuts a short vertical clip: webcam on top, gameplay below, burned-in subtitles and a caption.
  • Shows each video on a posting screen: preview, editable caption with credit and hashtags, privacy choice, comment/duet/stitch settings and content disclosure.
  • Posts to TikTok only after the creator presses “Post to TikTok” (TikTok Content Posting API, Direct Post).

Clip Studio is not affiliated with TikTok, Twitch, YouTube or Rockstar Games.
